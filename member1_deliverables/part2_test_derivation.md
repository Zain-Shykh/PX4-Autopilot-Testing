# Part 2 — Structural test derivation

The executable tests are the detailed setup/run/check record. [Sheet 1](sheet1_test_inventory.csv) maps each unique test ID to its controlled inputs, expected result, function, structural target, source line, and XML execution evidence. There are 36 new tests; none is copied or renamed from upstream. MC/DC remains Member 2's assigned critical component; this section does not claim estimator MC/DC.

For estimator initialization (`TC_M1_A01–A03`), the outer `_initialized`/`_data_good` decisions require a no-data case, valid vectors, and invalid normalization. A tilted gravity/magnetic vector pair has a known attitude, permitting a numerical oracle independent of merely checking that a function returns true. Zero acceleration and collinear magnetometer/gravity vectors exercise initialization rejection and subsequent recovery.

For `update()` (`A04–A13`), the tests distinguish vision, mocap, and magnetic fallback rather than accepting any finite quaternion. Constant gyro propagation has the analytic yaw `2*atan(rate*dt/2)`. Conflicting magnetic/vision headings prove source precedence. Spin at `0.873`, twice that threshold, and beyond the gain cap checks magnetic correction scaling. Acceleration at exactly `0.9g` and `1.1g` must be rejected by strict comparisons while the interior is accepted. Compensation subtracts the controlled body acceleration. Bias learning clips in both directions and stops at the strict `0.175 rad/s` threshold. NaN gyro and zero corrected acceleration require rollback to the previous quaternion and zeroed bias/rates.

`A11` deliberately injects `_bias_max` to test the downstream saturation algorithm. It does **not** prove parameter wiring. `A25` separately checks the real `ATT_BIAS_MAX` parameter path and characterizes the confirmed wiring defect; see Part 4.

Declination and parameter cases (`A14–A15`, `A26`) distinguish pre-initialization, negligible and material declination changes, forced versus notification-driven updates, absent magnetometer, and zero magnetic weight. Sensor/message cases (`A16–A22`) exercise independent gyro/acceleration timestamps, zero and exact-threshold sensor vectors, GPS enablement and strict accuracy threshold, both odometry validation paths, missing variance, excessive/negative variance, source modes, and exact freshness boundaries. Each gate in the six-term local-position acceptance decision is made false separately, with nonzero history proving that rejection clears all derivative state. First and repeated timestamps avoid differentiation; a known rotated velocity increment checks body-frame acceleration.

Publication/routing/startup cases (`A23–A24`, `A27–A29`) check publish suppression on failure, timestamp propagation, the 10-microsecond/20-millisecond timestep clamps, new-sample routing, duplicate estimator freshness, absence of another publisher, and stop handling. They call `Run()` synchronously; they do not test scheduler concurrency.

For hysteresis (`H01–H07`), the comparison `now >= request_time + delay` motivates deadline-minus-one and exact-deadline assertions in both directions. Cancellation at the old deadline distinguishes the order of requested-state handling from time advancement. Shortening/extending an already-pending delay verifies that the original request timestamp survives a duration change. Additional cases cover zero timestamp and zero delay, interleaved instances, and an exact deadline at the largest timestamp. Unsigned timestamp overflow and backwards clocks are outside the monotonic, non-overflowing operating assumptions; the large-time test does not claim to cover overflow.

The remaining source/branch obligations are investigated in [Part 3](part3_coverage_evidence.md) and [uncovered_obligations.csv](coverage/uncovered_obligations.csv). Finite normalized quaternion length guards and Boolean-impossible hysteresis edges are explained rather than bypassed by changing production logic.
